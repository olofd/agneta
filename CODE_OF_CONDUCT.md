# Code of conduct

Agneta's issues, pull requests and discussions are for making Agneta better. Everyone taking part, maintainers included, keeps them a place where people can do that.

## What's expected

- **Be considerate.** Assume good faith, disagree about the work and not the person, and accept a no gracefully, whichever side of it you're on.
- **Be precise.** Say what you ran, what you saw and what you expected. Measurements beat opinions, here as in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Respect people's time and privacy.** Keep threads on topic. Never post someone else's personal details, credentials, prompts or traces without their consent; redact your own before sharing (`agneta-diag export --redact-content`).

## What isn't accepted

Harassment in any form; insults, slurs and personal or political attacks; sexualized language or imagery; threats; deliberately disrupting discussions; and publishing others' private information. This applies whoever it's aimed at, and to private messages that follow from the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately. Open an issue titled "Conduct contact", with no details, and the maintainer will reply with a private way to send the report. Content on GitHub can also be reported to GitHub with **Report content**, in the menu of any comment.

Reports are kept confidential. The person reporting won't be named to the person reported without their consent.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what to do and may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests, lock a thread, or block someone from the project, temporarily or for good. Faced with the same conduct, the response is the same whoever is involved, including people who contribute often.
