Give Mac apps an AI agent you control.

Agneta is an open-source menu bar app for macOS. Connect the AI you already pay for once: a ChatGPT or Claude plan, or an OpenAI or Anthropic API key. Then decide which apps may use it. Agneta runs each task on your Mac, in a sandbox, on your account, and shows you every task with a stop button.

For you, with your own AI plan

Use the plan or key you already pay for in the apps you choose, see what each one does with it, and stop it.

  • An app has to ask first. You see the tools and folders it wants and whether its code signature checks out, then allow it once or always.
  • You choose which of your plans and API keys each app may use, and can remove its access at any time.
  • Every task shows the app it belongs to, the provider it runs on and what it costs.

For app developers

Agent features without running an agent loop, building sign-in for every provider, or putting a model bill on your own credit card.

  • Speak the Agent Client Protocol over a local WebSocket. No SDK is needed, and a sandboxed Mac App Store app can do it too.
  • Keep your tools and data in your app, served as an MCP server on loopback. The agent uses them on the user’s account.
  • Send a policy with each session: tools, folders, network, approval and limits. Agneta enforces it with sandboxes, or refuses it.
  • Usage comes back with every result, and every task leaves a full trace, live or later.

How it works

{{how_steps}}

What stays on your Mac

Agneta has no servers of its own and sends no telemetry. What leaves your Mac is the task itself: its prompts, and what its tools return, go to your AI provider over HTTPS, because that’s how the model sees it.

Your sign-ins
Plans are signed in by your own Codex or Claude Code, which keep and refresh their credentials. Agneta never holds a plan’s tokens, and API keys stay in your Keychain.
Loopback only
Agneta listens on 127.0.0.1 and refuses requests from web pages. Nothing listens beyond your Mac.
Sandboxed engines
Each engine runs under a macOS sandbox profile: the task’s workspace, the folders its policy names and what the engine itself needs, and nothing else in your home folder.
Commands, stricter still
Shell commands run confined to the workspace, with no network when the policy says none.
Traces you keep
Every task leaves a trace of its prompts, tool calls, model calls and decisions, readable only by you and kept 30 days by default.
Updates, once you allow them
Release builds ask before they first check GitHub for updates. The check carries Agneta’s version and nothing of yours.

Add it to your app

Your app is an ACP client and Agneta is the ACP agent. Connect to ws://127.0.0.1:47321/acp, pair once, and start sessions with your own tools and a policy. Any WebSocket and JSON-RPC 2.0 code will do.
